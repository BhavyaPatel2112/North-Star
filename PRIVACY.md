# North Star privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

North Star helps runners in Mumbai plan the best route and time to run. It is
built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no ads and no
tracking.

## What North Star uses, and why

**Your location** (only with your permission)
- Used to plan runs from where you are and to show conditions nearby.
- When you plan a run, the start point (and finish, if you choose one) is sent
  to the North Star route server to work out routes. It is used for that
  request only and is not stored or linked to you.

**Apple Health** (only with your permission)
- North Star reads your running workouts (date and distance) to move you along
  your journey towards your goal.
- It only reads. It never writes to Apple Health.
- Your health data stays on your phone. It is never sent to our server or to
  anyone else, and it is never used for advertising.

**Saved places and your goal**
- Places you save, your distance goal and your progress are stored only on
  your device.

## Services North Star relies on

- **Apple Maps** (on your device): place search, nearby cafes, stations and
  medical stores, and walking directions.
- **North Star route server** (hosted on Render): plans routes from the points
  you send. To check that a route can really be walked, the server asks
  Google's walking directions about the route's line. No personal details are
  sent.
- **Air quality and weather forecasts** are read from our database (Supabase).
  No personal details are sent.

## Children

North Star is not directed at children under 13.
